Website analytics and privacy

This page explains the limited analytics used to understand aggregate website usage and the presentation of my research.

This website uses privacy-preserving analytics to understand aggregate website usage and the presentation of my research. The analytics system records aggregate counts of website visits, pages viewed, referring domains, approximate geographic location, device category, link clicks, and file downloads.

Approximate country, region, and city may be determined from a visitor's IP address at the time of a request. IP addresses are not retained in the analytics datasets. The website does not use advertising trackers, cross-site tracking, device fingerprinting, or persistent identifiers designed to recognize visitors across visits.

Geographic analytics are retained in weekly aggregate form. Referral analytics are retained in daily aggregate form. Referring information is retained only at the domain or source-category level; complete referring URLs and search-query parameters are not stored.

Click and download information is retained only as aggregate counts, including interactions with research papers, abstracts, the curriculum vitae, academic-profile links, coauthor links, and other website resources. These analytics are not used for advertising or to create individual visitor profiles.

Automated traffic may be separately classified in order to distinguish human visits from search-engine crawlers, AI crawlers, monitoring services, and other automated requests. Technical information about automated traffic may include crawler identity, user-agent information, requested paths, request counts, network organization or ASN, approximate country, response status, and compliance with this website's robots.txt rules.

City-level analytics are retained only for the academic job-market period and are deleted after that period. Other analytics are retained only as necessary for website audience measurement, security, and administration.

For questions about this website or its analytics practices, contact federico.lenzi@kellogg.northwestern.edu.
